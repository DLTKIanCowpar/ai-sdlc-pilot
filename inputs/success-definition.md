## What Leadership Expects

**Faster delivery, not just training completed (Explicit)**
Dinakar's standard question is velocity: how much faster did delivery get, with a baseline,
measured on the same team. He wants one number.
ProPricer is the benchmark: ~4x story points with the same team, no added resources.
Bret's conclusion from the debrief: "Expect us to be measured against it."
A smaller number "needs an explanation ready."

**Teams genuinely working this way — not just using tools (Explicit)**
Lisa's "One Measurement for Every New-Feature Team" slide: "Tool use alone does not count."
Four tests for real adoption:
- **Context:** A knowledge base covers every domain the team works in
- **Connected:** PM, UX, Engineering, and QE all work from shared skills and agents
- **Gated:** Work from Framing through Verifying runs on the AI-SDLC, with people owning every gate
- **Measured:** Work feeds the productivity index and AI usage data

**Agents doing most of the work, humans supervising (Explicit)**
2027 goal: "the AI-SDLC runs at L3 in every core phase."
L3 = agents run the workflow; a person approves before it moves on.
Lisa's slide notes testing could reach L4/L5.

**A defensible results story for Roper (Explicit)**
Bret told Roper prep group (July): Deltek has not yet realized end-to-end benefits of a
true AI-powered SDLC. The rollout is expected to produce end-to-end proof. (Inferred)

## What a Failed Rollout Looks Like

**Explicit failure signals:**
- No velocity number, or a number well below ProPricer's 4x without an explanation
- People still acting as the trigger between stages (Dinakar: "will not accept humans as the trigger between gates"; accepts human gates, not human triggers)
- Counting tool installs or logins as adoption (ruled out explicitly in Lisa's slide)
- Managers not engaged (Lisa: survey data confirmed managers are not as engaged as needed)
- Setup blocking non-developer roles (Lisa flagged a product owner struggling to install the AI-SDLC)

**Inferred triggers for course correction:**
- Teams complete the camp but run no 2027.2 features through it
- January survey shows low or occasional use
- Reviewing AI-written specs slows teams down (most-cited pain in ProPricer readout)
- Maconomy remains near 10% after November

## Non-Negotiables

**Must:**
- People own every gate: nothing written to Aha, ADO, or Notion without explicit confirmation
- Never fabricate; ground before stating — answers come from help content, ADO wiki, live code
- Measure real adoption, not tool use
- Lock in a same-team baseline and agreed counting method before the first quarter of results

**Must not:**
- Use people as the hand-off trigger between stages (Dinakar's position — Explicit)
- Launch on content that is out of date with the repo (camp v2 prerequisite — Inferred from working group discussions)
- Require feature-team owners and scrum masters to attend every session (Inferred)
