AI-SDLC Enablement Glossary

A companion to the AI-SDLC Camp. Keep it open while you work: where you are in the six stages, what each stage produces, which artifacts live outside the camp, and what the words mean. Examples are Vantagepoint's, as in the camp. Fill in your product's names in the yellow cells.

1. Stage tracker

Six stages, one chain of artifacts. Pick your role to highlight your stages, click a stage for detail, and mark stages as you finish them.

Progress and notes live on this page only while it is open. Use the summary button to save them somewhere you keep.

2. Role cards

The seven quick cards from Module 5, in full: what changes for you, the phrases you type, the checklists, your first week, and the questions the team has already answered. Tick checklist items as you go. Commands and record numbers are Vantagepoint's.

3. Board columns

Framing and Feature Review happen before the board, on the Feature. The Kanban board tracks PBIs in eight columns once the release plan is defined. Column names are the process vocabulary; check your board for the state names your team uses.

Before the board: Proposed, then Framing, then Feature Review, then Release plan defined.

4. Artifacts and places

The things you will make, find or be asked about. Items marked Outside the camp live in another system, so the camp can only point at them. Items marked Described in camp are explained in the modules.

5. Your product's systems

The camp uses generic names so the process reads the same everywhere. Vantagepoint's instances are shown. Fill in the yellow cells for your product. Click a cell to type.
